Privacy policy · effective October 19, 2026

What the game knows about you

Nothing it sends to us. Hollow Sun has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and the developer receives no data about you.

Kept on your device

Which missions you have won, your difficulty and sound settings, the clan you played last and your skirmish choices. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

Online skirmish uses Apple's Game Center to find an opponent. When you choose Live opponent, Game Center matches you with another player and the two devices exchange game moves through Game Center for the length of the battle; your opponent sees your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can sign out of it in your device's Settings; the campaign and skirmish against the computer work without it.

Purchases

War charters are sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the game only that the purchase exists. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

Tracking

None. The game contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and does not use App Tracking Transparency.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
