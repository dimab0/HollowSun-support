Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

The short version

Hollow Sun does not collect any data.

What is stored on your device

Your campaign progress and your settings (difficulty, sound, pause options). Deleting the app removes them. Nothing is uploaded: the game has no server and makes no network requests of its own.

Purchases

Campaign unlocks are sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment; the game receives only confirmation that the purchase exists. We never see your name, email or payment details.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. The game contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
