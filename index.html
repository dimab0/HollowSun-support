Three clans. One dying star.

Hollow Sun is a sci-fi base-building strategy game for iPhone and iPad, made for touch. Pause whenever you like, give your orders, and command at your own pace.

The story

Nine hundred years ago something began building a shell around the star Ilos, and then vanished with the work unfinished. Now the star is going out, and three peoples are fighting over the one machine that still answers.

  • Meridian CompactArmoured humans who want to relight the star.
  • The ChoirThe builders’ own machines, who want to finish the shell.
  • The VerdanceA living swarm that wants to leave before the dark.

The game

  • A base centre, five structures, five units and an upgrade tree for every clan
  • Fifteen missions: a five-mission campaign per clan
  • A tactical pause, and a battle that waits while you build
  • The first two missions of every clan are free. No ads, no timers, no account
  • Works offline
