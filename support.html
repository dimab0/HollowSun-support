Support

Email braznichenko@gmail.com and you will get an answer from the person who made the game.

How do I control my units?

Tap a unit or structure to select it. Tap the ground to send the selection there; fighters engage anything they meet on the way. Tap an enemy to attack it. Tap a selected unit a second time to select every unit of its kind on screen. The Army button selects every fighter, and Box lets you drag a selection frame.

How do I build?

Tap the orange Build button, choose a structure, drag it to open ground you can see, and tap Build here. Structures raise themselves. To train units, select a structure and tap a unit on its card.

It is too fast for me

Pause at any time with the button in the top left corner: you can still select, give orders and build while paused. The battle also freezes by itself while the build menu is open. Before each mission you can choose Relaxed difficulty, which sends smaller attacks further apart.

What is free and what is paid?

The first two missions of every clan are free. Each clan’s full campaign (missions 3 to 5) is a separate one-time purchase. There are no subscriptions, ads or consumable purchases.

I bought a campaign and it is locked again

Tap Restore purchases on the title screen, or Restore on the clan’s mission list, while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Does my progress sync between devices?

Purchases follow your Apple Account. Campaign progress is stored on the device and is not synced.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
