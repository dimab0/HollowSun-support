Commanding

How do I move and attack?

Tap a unit or a structure to select it, then tap the ground: the selection marches there and fights whatever it meets on the way. Tap an enemy to attack it. Tap a selected unit again to pick up every unit of its kind on screen. Army selects every fighter; Box lets you drag a frame.

How do I build and train?

Tap the orange Build button, choose a structure, move it onto open ground you can see, and tap Build here. Structures raise themselves. To train, select a structure and tap a unit on its card. A queue can be as long as your cinder and supply allow; its progress shows on the map over the structure.

It moves too fast for me

The pause button, top left, stops the battle; you can still select, order and build while paused. The battle also freezes while the build menu is open. Relaxed difficulty, chosen on each briefing, sends smaller attacks further apart.

Skirmish and online

How do I play another person?

Skirmish > Live opponent. Game Center pairs you with someone who wants the same map size right now. Online battles run in real time without pause. You need to be signed in to Game Center on the device.

War charters

What is free and what is paid?

Missions 1 and 2 of every clan are free. Each clan's war charter ($2.99, bought once) opens that clan's missions 3 to 5. Owning any charter opens skirmish and live battles. There are no subscriptions, ads or consumables.

My charter shows as missing

Tap Recover charters on the first screen, or Recover on the clan's mission list, while signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

Does progress follow me to another device?

Charters follow your Apple Account. Campaign progress stays on the device it was played on.
